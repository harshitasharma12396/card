# Surface brief: wedding invitation (src/app/page.tsx)

Scope: one-page mobile-first invitation site. Visitor mode: Experience (the invitation itself leads).
Audience: wedding guests opening a WhatsApp link on a phone. Job: learn every event's time and place, and RSVP.
Proof/content: real couple, five-day schedule, families and 15 photos supplied by the family (translated from their printed Hindi card); art is placeholder SVG; no music file yet.
Constraints: English only; one content file; no fabricated real-looking facts; reduced-motion path for every animation.
Memorable moment: tapping a gold wax seal opens a glowing envelope onto the invitation.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Opening the invitation is the ritual. A sealed envelope on a carved pedestal opens into one continuous dusk-lit scroll of ornate Indian frames; it refuses the generic "hero + cards + form" wedding template by letting each section be a different physical object (envelope, arched card, scroll-and-tassel cards, framed paintings, torana countdown).

OWN-WORLD: Dusk-sky pink to peach to lavender gradient ground; blush-ivory paper cards; terracotta-rose frame lines and antique gold ornament; deep plum text and buttons. Arches, jharokha frames, hanging chandeliers/umbrellas, bead tassels, torana, lanterns and petals, all drawn as authored SVG. Gilda Display for names and headings, Instrument Serif italic for accents, Figtree for small labels and form text, Tiro Devanagari Hindi for the blessing. Recognizable with the words removed by its arches, tassels and gold-on-blush palette.

STORY: The guest feels invited personally, learns who/when/where for each ceremony, and replies in under a minute; the countdown and wish lantern leave a warm last note.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-height dusk sky, a pale envelope centered on a carved stone pedestal with a palace silhouette behind, floating petals, a gold wax seal at the envelope's centre with a slow pulsing halo, the words "Tap the seal to open" below it in small tracked caps, and a quiet "Skip" pill bottom-right. The seal is the only primary action.

FORM: Pinned by the user's reference video (not a rolled direction). Position on list: n/a. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
